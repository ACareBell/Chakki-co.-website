    Frequently asked questions

    Everything you need to know about fresh chakki atta

    What is chakki atta?

    Chakki atta is whole wheat flour ground using traditional stone mills (chakkis), which preserves the bran, germ, and endosperm for maximum nutrition and flavour — unlike roller-milled packaged flour.

    How is Chakki & Co. atta different from packaged atta?

    We mill your atta fresh per order using emery stone grinding. There are no preservatives, no maida mixing, and no additives — just 100% whole wheat or your chosen grain blend, ground the same day it ships.

    Do you offer home delivery across India?

    Yes, Chakki & Co. delivers freshly milled atta pan-India with fast dispatch. Free delivery is available on orders above ₹499.

    What is multigrain atta made of?

    Our multigrain atta blend includes whole wheat, barley, ragi (finger millet), chana (chickpea flour), oats, and soya — providing higher fibre and protein than plain wheat atta.

    Can I order a custom grain blend?

    Yes! Chakki & Co. lets you customise your own atta blend from a range of whole grains. Your blend is then stone-ground fresh and delivered to your door.
